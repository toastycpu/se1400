The Audience:

Geolocation

The retro tech and modding community is worldwide, but based on my experience it appears to be most popular in the United States.

Age

The primary audience ranges from early teens to late 40s. Younger users are drawn to modding as a hobby, while older users tend to be nostalgic for the technology they grew up with.

Gender

From my experience I noticed that it seems to be more of male-dominated hobby, but Analog Diaries aims to make the content more accessible and welcoming to women and beginners of all backgrounds.

Other

The audience is typically made up of hobbyists and collectors. Many are beginners looking for a starting point, which is exactly who this site is built for.
