The Competitors:

YouTube Channels

There are countless tutorial videos available, but quality is hit or miss. Some are helpful while others waste your time. Instead of sifting through dozens of videos, Analog Diaries will provide focused tutorials based on my own journey and the exact steps I took/resources I used.

Reddit

Reddit is good for finding resources and community support, but it can very overwhelming with too many conflicting opinions and no clear consensus. Analog Diaries will offer my personal experience with each mod, including what to look out for and how to find reliable sellers when buying items.

iFixit

iFixit is one of the best resources for hardware tutorials and tools, but it lacks personality since it's company owned. Analog Diaries will bring a real personal experience from someone who isn't a skilled modder and doing it for a hobby.
