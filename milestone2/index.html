Website Summary:

What content will the site contain?

It will be a blog style site about restoring and modding old tech, and documenting the experience with bringing vintage devices to life.

Where will you get all of the content for your site(ie. images & text)?

I will use own photos and writeups from my experience on the items that I have modded this summer or have bought.

Why should users care about the focus of the site?

Many people have become frustrated with modern tech and are longing for simpler times. This site offers an escape and a way to reconnect with old tech to live an analog life.

Why should a site be built for this?

Getting started with modding can be daunting. Finding good resources are scattered and hard to find. People also love following the journey and experiences of others.

What would you like users to do at your site?

Learn from my experiences, discover helpful tools and resources, and find out how to make use of old tech they already have sitting around their house.

What is the goal of the site?

To share my hobby and document my journey of becoming more analog. While also providing help to others that are looking into or need help starting a similar journey.
