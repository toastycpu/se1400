What are we about?

I created this site as a love letter to old tech. Through two ways one reviving old tech and two using it to feel alive again. I hope to help others discover tools and resources for modding devices, so it doesn't feel so daunting. Through sharing my hobby and documenting my journey of becoming more analog, I want to provide help to others who are looking into or need help starting a similar journey of their own.

Living Analog

In a world that has become overwhelming and so distant, taking a break from the noise and living in the moment is something that we can take back. I want to go back to using tech to benefit us, not the other way around. Saying goodbye to overpriced subscriptions and marketing algorithms designed to keep us scrolling. Let's start by living in the moment, one meaningful gadget at a time.

What is Modding?

If this is the first time you've heard the word mod, it's the ability to change the hardware or software of a device to make it fit your own preferences. Opening up a device isn't as scary as it may seem. If you take it slow, follow a good tutorial, and aren't afraid to make a few mistakes along the way, modding can become one of the most rewarding hobbies you pick up. It's a hands-on activity that gives you a understand of the tech that is used every day, instead of just consuming it.
