If you are interested in joining a community of vintage tech enthusiasts, need more help with modding and restoring devices, or just want to live a more analog life, feel free to reach out and grow your passion with us.

FAQ

  • Do I need experience with modding to join? Of course not. We welcome complete beginners just as much as people you've had experince working with old tech. We want to build a safe environment where seasoned veterans and newbies feel free to teach and learn from one another.
  • Is there a cost to join the club? There's no hidden fees, it's completely free. Just fill out the form below, we'll review it and reach out soon. Donations are much appreciated.
  • What kind of devices does this community focus on? Anything analog or older-tech, such as digital cameras, music players, handheld consoles, camcorders, and more. If it's got a slower lifestyle than a smartphone or modern tech, it probably fits here. Getting started with customizing and modding can be intimidating, which is why we created this site. Somewhere to bring your device, make it uniquely yours, and get a little help if you need it. Anything that makes you step away from the loud noise of the world and live in the moment, is the perfect canidate.
  • How will I hear back after signing up? We'll reach out by email with more info once you submit the form. There we will send you a link to the discord community. We will also answer any question you might have by the email as will.

Thanks for joining the Club! We'll reach out soon.
