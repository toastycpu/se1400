Joy-Con Shell Swap

I really wanted the Animal Crossing Joy-Cons during the pandemic, but they sold out very quickly and wouldn't restock. So I decided to buy a regular Switch and make it look the way I always wanted.

Finding a seller: the first step of swapping Joy-Con shells is finding a reliable seller. I'd recommend sites such as: eXtremeRate, Etsy, and BASSTOP but always check the reviews before purchasing for any seller.

Tools: I recommend the iFixit Moray Driver Kit-32. You'll need the Y000 and a +000 screw head for this mod. They are also magnetic so that they are easier to pull out.

The hardest part: The first 4 screws are the toughest to get out, they aren't magnetic. Apply firm and steady pressure straight down onto the screw head. Make sure there is no tilting in the driver. If it doesn't budge after 2 attempts, take a break. This is the hardest part it might strip the screw head. If that happens, grab a flathead screwdriver (- 0) close to the length of the Y000 and apply pressure to remove it. Take it slow and you'll need to buy new screws. I got mine from amazon and it came with all the extra scews I would ever need just in chance.

iPod 5.5 Mod

My brother had the Ipod Classic while we were growing up. This is one of the major ways I discovered music. I finally found it again in an old box, and I had all the old feels back again. It turned on after a charge but the aux was not working but it was still working just needed a little bit of love.

Finding a seller: The two biggest Ipod product seller that I found was Elite Obsolute Electronic (us based) and iDemiGods (canada based).

Tools: I recommend the iFixit Moray Driver Kit-32. You will need a +000 screw head for this mod depending on the parts you are swapping you'll need more screw heads. Which should be included in this kit.

The hardest part: Out of all the Ipod classics the 5.5 and 5 are the easiest to open but they are very hard to open without damiging the shell. Be very gentle and slow there is a lot of thin wiring.
